# NeoSlides

A presentation program: slides with text, images and shapes placed with the mouse, layout templates, themes, transitions, speaker notes and a full-screen presentation mode. Your presentations are saved automatically, synced between your PCs and can be exported to PDF.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Your presentations

- **+ New** creates a presentation, saved automatically on every change.
- **Open…** imports a NeoSlides file (.neoslides).
- The list can be filtered with **Search**. Each presentation can be **renamed**, **duplicated** or **deleted** (deleting also applies on your other synced PCs).
- A presentation changed on another PC updates here, and the plugin tells you.

## Slides

- **+ Slide** adds a slide from a template: title, title and text, two columns, image and text, quote, key figures, outline, section, "Thank you / Any questions?" or blank.
- The left column shows all the slides: click to go to one, drag to reorder, **duplicate** or **delete**. With no element selected, **↑**/**↓** or **Page Up**/**Page Down** move between slides.
- **Speaker notes**: one text per slide, visible only in the editor.
- **Transition** when each slide appears: none, fade, slide or zoom.

## Elements

- **Text box**: double-click to type. Smaller or larger text, bold, italic, alignment, colour.
- **Image**: from a file, from a link, or pasted with Ctrl+V after copying it.
- **Shape**: rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, triangle, arrow, star, heart, diamond, speech bubble or line, with its colour.
- **Move** and **resize** with the mouse. An image keeps its proportions (**Shift** frees them); for other elements it is the reverse: **Shift** keeps the proportions.
- The **arrow keys** move the selected element by 2 px (10 px with **Shift**); **Ctrl+D** duplicates it.
- **Place**: centre horizontally or vertically, align top, bottom, left or right.
- **Order**: bring to front or send to back.
- **Delete** or **Backspace** removes the selected element.

## Themes and background

Ten colour and font themes: Neon, White, Night, Dawn, Ocean, Forest, Pink, Mono, Slate and Paper. Each slide can also have its own background.

## Presenting

**▶ Present** (or **F5**), from the start or from the current slide (**Shift+F5**), goes full screen:

- next slide: **→**, **↓**, **Space**, **Enter**, **Page Down** or click;
- a click on the left quarter of the screen goes back;
- previous: **←**, **↑**, **Page Up** or **Backspace**;
- **Home** and **End**: first and last slide;
- **Escape**: exit.

## Export

**Export** to PDF (one page per slide), or to a NeoSlides file to share or keep it.

## Data and sync

Your presentations are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. On first launch, the plugin picks up what you already had in NeoDex.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → NeoSlides → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
